# Content Strategy Guide

## Brand Voice
- Professional yet warm (family business)
- Clinical accuracy without jargon
- Local community focus (Ilford, Essex, London)
- Trust-building, never salesy

## Content Pillars

### 1. Hearing Health (40%)
- Hearing aid guides, technology updates, tinnitus, workplace protection
- Target: 80+ articles

### 2. Eye Care (40%)
- Eye tests, lens technology, frame guides, UV protection, children's vision
- Target: 80+ articles

### 3. Wellness (20%)
- Nutrition, diabetes, sleep, senior care, seasonal health
- Target: 40+ articles

## Publishing Schedule
- **2 articles/week** for first 3 months (build to 50+)
- **1 article/week** ongoing maintenance
- **Quarterly** long-form guides (2000+ words)

## Local SEO Actions
1. Google Business Profile — complete all fields, weekly posts
2. Local citations — Yell, Thomson Local, NHS directory
3. NAP consistency — match website exactly everywhere
4. Local link building — Ilford community groups, Essex health directories
5. Client reviews — encourage Google reviews post-appointment

## Content Expansion Path (22 → 200+ pages)

Current: 22 blog articles + 12 static pages = 34 indexed URLs

To reach 200+:
1. Add location pages (e.g., "Hearing Aids Romford", "Eye Tests Barkingside") — 30 pages
2. Expand blog to 150+ articles — content calendar above
3. Service sub-pages (each hearing aid type, lens type) — 20 pages
4. FAQ pages per service — 10 pages
5. Case studies / success stories — 15 pages

## Social Media Integration
- Share each blog post on Facebook and Instagram
- YouTube: embed hearing test and lens technology videos
- WhatsApp: appointment booking CTA in posts
